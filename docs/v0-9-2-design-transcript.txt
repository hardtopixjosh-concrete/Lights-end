Aggression · Bleeding · Mobility
Get into combat quickly, maintain pressure, and eliminate groups before they overwhelm you.
RMB - Savage Rush
Rush toward the cursor, cutting through enemies in a straight path. Damages multiple targets, applies Bleed,

180 damage per strike · 9-second cooldown

For 6 seconds, gain a powerful rage state. Manually performed LMB attacks create secondary spectral cuts. You
still have to attack manually; the ultimate enhances your actions rather than automatically fighting.
6-second duration · 40-second cooldown
Example combat sequence
Savage Rush into a pack → Carnage → Bloodlust → finish tougher targets with weapon-based LMB.

Balance and implementation notes
The listed damage and cooldown numbers are prototype targets. The reference medium demon has 1,000 HP
and the baseline Longsword deals 250 damage per normal LMB hit (four hits to defeat it).
All four paths work with every Warrior weapon family: Twin Blades, Throwing Axe, Greatsword, Longsword,
Halberd, and Warhammer. Do not bind secondary abilities to the current LMB weapon animation.
