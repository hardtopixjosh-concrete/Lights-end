MAGE CLASS
Implementation Companion | Version 0.2
A class-specific build specification for Codex, designed to supplement Action Roguelite Complete Game
Design Review V0.8 without replacing the shared game systems.
Authority rule: V0.8 remains the source of truth for world design, persistence, weapon rarity, forge,
enemies and events. This PDF defines Mage-specific controls, resources, skills, weapons and class balance.
Where older Mage brainstorming differs, the Arcane Energy system in this document supersedes it.
1 | Design identity and non-negotiable rules
• Mage is a ranged, manually controlled spellcaster, built for elemental combinations, positional play and
 burst/recovery rhythms.
• Player input: WASD movement, mouse aiming, LMB/RMB/Q/E/R combat, SPACE Blink, I inventory, F
 interaction.
• No automatic primary spells. Holding LMB repeats a free projectile; secondary spell effects may trigger
 from player-initiated actions.
• One equipped weapon at a time; no armor/jewelry; no weapon mastery XP. Permanent weapons and gold,
 temporary character level and skill allocations.
• Leveling grants automatic character stat growth. Eligible mini-boss victories award skill points; acquiring
 points does not open a menu. Spend them when convenient.

5 | Skill points, skill tree and evolution framework
The Mage uses a 24-node proposal: eight nodes per branch (six regular and two major evolutions). All nodes
cost one point. Up to eight eligible mini-boss rewards provide points per run. At this stage a maximum of two
major evolutions per run is the recommended balance target, not a separately approved choice.
Each major evolution needs at least three prior points invested in its branch and its named prerequisite. A
spell can have only one active replacement evolution at a time. Points persist only during the current run and
may be respeced at the fixed forge.

6 | Skill tree user experience
• No popups on XP level-ups and no forced mini-boss reward screens. Show a small available-points indicator.
 
• Allow skill points to accumulate. Tree can open on demand while the single-player simulation is paused.
 
• Represent each branch with a compact connected path; display prerequisites, active effects, energy-cost
 changes and conflicts before confirming selection.
• Never require spending points to access basic RMB/Q/E/R skills; all starting controls function at level 1.

All Mage weapons use the same rarity order as V0.8: Common (gray), Magic (blue), Rare (purple), Legendary
(orange), Mythic (gold), Ascendant (red). Each owns a unique persistent ID, affixes, rarity, saved item-level
ceiling, forging state, cosmetic identity and signature effects.
Critical scaling rule
At the start of each ordinary new run, effective weapon level = 1. The numerical base damage and affix
magnitudes increase with character level until they reach the weapon's saved item-level ceiling. The weapon
always keeps its rarity, model/VFX identity, affix types, and signature behavior. Numeric proc strength scales;
visual signature effects do not disappear. The item itself gains no weapon XP or mastery.

Affixes and the forge
• Mage-specific affixes: spell/elemental damage, cast speed, max energy, energy regeneration, Fireball AoE,
 Nova radius, lightning targets, Meteor damage, Blink cooldown, reaction damage, status duration and boss
damage.
• Add class/family eligibility tags to the existing shared affix generator. Restrict incompatible affixes and
 prevent duplicated unique signature effects.
• Use the existing fixed-location forge and shared gold economy: ordinary affix rerolls, +quality, salvage and
 current-run respec. Do not introduce a Mage-exclusive currency.
• Rerolls cannot create Sovereign-exclusive signature effects. Realm weapons are obtained only through the
 realm boss.

10 | Class parity and performance acceptance
The Mage should not fall behind the Swordsman due to energy downtime. Benchmark both classes with
equivalent effective-level and rarity weapons under similar skill investment.
Metric
Initial acceptance hypothesis
Single-target boss output
Over representative 60-second rotations, approximately within 10-15% sustained
damage at similar build investment.
Wave clear
Comparable defeated-enemies-per-minute outcomes; Mage favors ranged burst,
Swordsman consistent cleave.
Survivability
Similar skilled-player run completion; Mage has lower HP offset by ranged control
and Blink.
Resource feel
Free Arcane Bolt always viable; multiple costly spells can be chained, followed by a
short rapid recovery period.
Browser performance
Shared visual/projectile pooling; capped chain bounces, reactions, damage numbers
and overlap queries.
11 | Codex build instructions and order
• 1. Audit the existing project and confirm framework, actual sprite animations and established shared
 services; do not overwrite the Swordsman.
• 2. Implement Mage controller with eight-direction mouse-facing and existing camera. Map keys to
 class-specific spell actions.
• 3. Implement a reusable Arcane Energy component with staged regeneration, costs, pause rules, UI, and
 automated tests.
• 4. Add Arcane Bolt, Fireball, Frost Nova, Chain Lightning, Meteor and Blink using reusable
 projectile/area/chain/status building blocks.
• 5. Add controlled elemental reactions and global status-resistance/crowd-control parity with bosses.
 
• 6. Implement data-driven 24-node Mage tree, per-ability evolution restrictions, mini-boss points, deferred
 spending and respec.
• 7. Add staff/wand/spellbook weapon families and Mage-specific affix filtering without duplicating inventory
 or persistence systems.
• 8. Verify level-1 re-entry scaling, saved ceiling behavior, realm-exclusive effects, forging and death/restart
 persistence.
• 9. Test major bosses, Blood Moon, trials, elites, and secret Sovereign realm with both Mage and
 Swordsman.
• 10. Report changed files, test evidence, observed FPS and unresolved balance questions, rather than
 assuming values are finalized.

12 | Decisions not yet locked
• Maximum of two evolutions per run (recommended, not confirmed).
 
• The precise internal node prerequisite topology beyond the named evolution prerequisites.
 
• Whether Arcane Energy can exceed 100 from an affix or node and how stages interact with bonuses.
 
• Exact cast timings and hit frames based on the asset pack actually in the Codex folder.
 
• All damage numbers, energy values, cooldowns and affix rolls need measured playtesting.
 
Final consistency check: No conventional mana bar; no Arcane Momentum; no automatic basic attacks; no
skill point per character level; no weapon mastery; no permanent armor; no reset of weapon identity/effects;
no lethal permanent run-ending result from a defeat inside the Sovereign realm.
