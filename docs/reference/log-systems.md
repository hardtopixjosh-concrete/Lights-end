## Oct 19 fx-256 — lobby fps cycling: measured cause and proven fix

User: menu "great, then ____ of low fps, then great", for a long time, local and online; earlier fixes were unmeasured guesses.

- Findings: title screen was 120 fps live and local (not the problem). Lobby (fe-loadout) on both live and local: 120 fps with 3–5 s stretches at 30 fps every ~10 s (live 4/min, local 19 of 60 s below 60 fps). In slow stretches both the game loop and front-end drawPreviews jumped from ~3/2.4 ms to ~16 ms per frame together (GPU sync stalls). Skipping drawPreviews removed every slow second (0/60).
