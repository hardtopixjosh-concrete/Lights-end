# Ranger build log

## October 17 — fx-144: Ranger class, four specializations (first playable batch)

Design source: the user's pasted Ranger design and their gap answers (see decisions.md, Oct 17 "New class: Ranger"). All numbers are Claude's proposals.

**Actual behavior**
- Third class on Select character / Prepare Run (key 3). One body for every spec: the 2Archer sprite from `2D HD Character pack 1 V1.2.zip`, 14 clips prepared by `sites/veyrath/prepare-ranger-assets.py` into `dist/assets/ranger-*.webp` (~1.1 MB). Spec colour is a neon ground glow under the body plus coloured projectiles (Archer green, Ninja purple, Alchemist teal, Gunslinger gold). Max HP 200 (Mage 170, Warrior 250).
- **LMB = Bow weapon family** (family 12, `primary:'bow'`) for every spec: hold to fire arrows at the cursor, 100 damage every 0.42 s (weapon speed/reach/projectile affixes apply; Velocity affix can roll on bows; no bow behavior affixes yet). Starter "Worn Bow" is granted to existing saves by `migrateArmory`.
- **Momentum** 0–100, starts empty, +5 per bow hit (clone copies don't count), decays 6/s after 4 s without a hit. Skills show "short" when you can't afford them; casting without enough shows a toast.
- **Shared statuses:** Marked +15% damage taken from the Ranger (gold reticle), Exposed +25% (purple broken ring). Ranger DoTs: burn 14 per 0.5 s for 3 s, poison 6 per stack per 0.5 s (max 5 stacks), frost slows.
- **Space = Roll** (the Warrior's dash with the archer Rolling clip). Ninja Smoke Bomb's "untargetable" is implemented as 1.5 s invulnerable + translucent body; enemies keep chasing (no true aggro drop).
- **Archer:** RMB Power Shot hold-to-draw (aim line, 0.6× move while drawing), release fires a pierce-all arrow 260→640 by draw (1 s full). Q Arrow Type cycles Fire / Frost / Poison (default Fire once Q is learned; plain before), applied to Quick Shot, Power Shot and Volley; slot name shows the current type. E Volley: 6 waves over 1.8 s on a 150-radius circle at the cursor, 55 each, Marks 4 s. R Hunter's Focus 6 s: 2.2× fire rate, arrows pierce 2 more, +25% crit, Quick Shot auto-aims at the nearest Marked enemy within 750.
- **Ninja:** E Shuriken 0.3 s / 8 Momentum, 90 damage, 2 bounces, ×1.3 vs Exposed. RMB Cyclone 12 blades all around (110), a blade hitting an Exposed enemy ricochets once. Q Smoke Bomb 170 radius for 4 s Exposes enemies inside, 3 s +40% speed. R Shadow Assault 5 s: 3 clones dash to random enemies every 0.35 s (120 per strike); every bow arrow you fire is copied from each clone ("kunai" read as the bow LMB until a kunai weapon exists).
- **Alchemist:** RMB Catalyst Flask (0.25 s / 6) lobs a small flask; reaction by what it lands on: burning or fire puddle → Explosion (2.5×, ignites), poisoned or toxic cloud → Poison Burst (+25 per stack, +2 stacks), slowed or frost puddle → Shatter (2×, slow, short stagger), otherwise Ignite. E Volatile Mixture: toxic cloud 160 radius for 5 s (poison stack + slow every 0.5 s). Q Reactive Flask: 120 barrier for 4 s, knockback + 80 damage in 180, leaves a frost puddle. R Grand Experiment: every puddle explodes (300, by type), every poisoned/burning enemy within 900 bursts, then 6 s of enhanced reactions (×1.5 radius, ×1.6 damage, Explosion and Poison Burst together).
- **Gunslinger:** RMB Fan the Hammer 6 shots over ~0.5 s (70 each); vs Marked +50% and a ricochet bullet (50%); also ricochets off enemies with any other status. E Piercing Line hitscan 900 range, 350 through everything, Marks 4 s. Q Quickstep 190 dash with 0.5 s invulnerability along your move direction (or aim); every other ability cuts its cooldown by 0.5 s. R Bullet Barrage 4 s stream (45 per bullet every 0.06 s) toward the cursor at 0.7× move speed.
- Costs/cooldowns (RMB/Q/E/R): Archer 15/0/25/50, 1/0.35/1.5/10 s; Ninja 20/20/8/50, 1/4/0.3/10; Alchemist 6/20/25/50, 0.25/4/1.5/10; Gunslinger 15/10/20/50, 1/4/1.2/10.
- **64 spell behaviors** (4 per spell, same tier rules as other classes), all Claude proposals, listed in `talent-data.js` under archer/ninja/alchemist/gunslinger.
- Path unlocks follow the existing codex rule: the first path (Archer) is free, the others cost 600 gold each in Unlocks.

**Files:** new `ranger.js` (simulation), `ranger-render.js` (Canvas visuals), `prepare-ranger-assets.py`, `tests/ranger-smoke.mjs`; changed `game.js`, `specializations.js`, `talent-data.js`, `weapon-families.js`, `weapons.js`, `combat.js` (`c.rangerCrit`), `combat-balance.js` (bow), `affix-data.js`, `front-end.js`, `index.html`, `style.css` (three class cards). One live browser pass on localhost:8765: no console errors; Ranger selectable, Archer run starts with MOMENTUM bar and Archer slot names; level-1 offer lists Volley / Arrow Type / Power Shot; holding LMB built 20 Momentum; max HP 200. No screenshots; Ninja/Alchemist/Gunslinger visuals and feel not checked in the browser.

## October 17 — fx-157: Falconer's Glove, Shotgun, Chakram (user)

Families 19–21, loot for any Ranger spec at the same odds as the others.
- **Falconer's Glove** (140 per hit): one hawk at a time. LMB sends it at the cursor (up to 600); it hits every enemy it flies through (whole-model hitbox, 16 padding), then curves back to a random catch spot 70–130 from you, marked on the ground with a golden ring and an inner ring closing as it arrives (white ring when you're inside). Standing inside the 45 radius when it lands = caught: hawks fly at the 3 nearest enemies within 700 for +50% (210) and need no catching. Missed: feathers scatter and the hawk is back on the glove 0.6 s later. Out 0.45 s + back 0.55 s, faster with attack speed / Hunter's Focus. Shadow clones don't copy the hawk.
- **Shotgun:** 5 pellets in a 30° cone, 60 each, range 320, every 0.95 s. At most 3 pellets of one blast register on the same enemy; the other pellets pass through it (they can hit enemies behind).
- **Chakram:** 90 per pass, flies out 450 and homes back to you, piercing everything (each enemy once per pass); up to 2 in the air; one throw every 0.55 s.
- Velocity affix can roll on all three. Archer arrow types, Focus pierce and clone copies (except the hawk) apply.

Check: `node tests/ranger-smoke.mjs` passes, with new checks: shotgun registers 1–3 pellets on one enemy, chakrams hit and return, a caught hawk looses 3 follow-ups that hit, a missed catch looses none. Not checked in the browser. All numbers are proposals.

## October 17 — fx-158: weapon VFX overhaul (user brief)

New shader bridge `ranger-vfx.js` + 18 `rgr.*` recipes in vfx-recipes.js (tunable in the VFX Lab), drawn in the ground and upper passes after the Canvas layer. Palettes per subclass (Archer green, Ninja purple, Alchemist teal, Gunslinger gold; main/core/spark/smoke/dark), and Archer arrow types swap only the spark/core colours.
- **Projectiles, one look per weapon:** Bow/Short Bow arrow (hot head, coloured tail, trailing sparks, ground light pool); Long Bow (longer double tail, wake of motes); Crossbow bolt (thick tail, pulsing pierce ring at the head); Shuriken (spinning glints round a hot core + crisp white star); Kunai (thin streak, cutting glint, blade shape); Pistols/Gunslinger bullets (white-hot tracer, coloured halo); Shotgun pellets (small slugs, short tails); Chakram (spinning blade ring; **returning** pass switches to a brighter white-core ring with a homing streak); Power Shot (scaled by draw, pulsing rings, heavy sparks); hawk (aura, wake, feather sparks around the Canvas body). Shadow-clone copies are the Ninja purple at 60% and 75% size.
- **Release:** gun muzzle flash (forward flare, flash, sparks) for Pistols and bullets; Shotgun blast (big flare, smoke puff, cone of sparks, one per blast); bow/throw snap (flash + ring) for bows, thrown weapons and the hawk launch, larger for Crossbow / Long Bow / Power Shot. Multishot (Shuriken 3-fan, Split Shot) shows one snap per projectile.
- **Impacts:** a flash, a directional mark and sparks sprayed along the shot on every direct hit (not DoTs), scaled by damage (power = damage/160). **Piercing** hits that carry on show a thin streak through the enemy and a forward spray instead. **Crits:** ×1.3 impact plus a star flash (crossed beams), a double ring and a full spark burst, and a small camera shake.
- **Canvas:** with shaders on, Canvas draws only crisp shapes (arrow shafts and fletching, star, chakram blades, kunai) and its own glows/particles are skipped; with shaders off, the previous Canvas look remains (basic shots now use the subclass colour unless an Archer arrow type is active).
- **Crowd budget:** impacts capped at 36 and muzzle flashes at 30 alive; past 90 projectiles on screen every shot except Power Shot switches to a two-layer `rgr.lite` look.

Mechanics and balance unchanged. Checks: `node tests/ranger-smoke.mjs` passes; new `node tests/ranger-vfx-smoke.mjs` renders every Ranger family × subclass through the shader bridge (stub sink) and both Canvas modes with no errors, producing muzzles and impacts for each. A browser load on fx-158 showed no console errors, but the pane was hidden, so the game loop didn't run: **the look on screen has not been seen**. Skill visuals (Volley, smoke, flasks, explosions and so on) are unchanged Canvas.
