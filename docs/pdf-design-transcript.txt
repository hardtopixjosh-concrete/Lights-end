02 World, map, presentation & assets
Confirmed direction: one very large, seamless, mostly handcrafted isometric desert. Match the provided screenshot:
sand, vegetation, boulders, ruins, towering stones and natural open combat spaces. No forced region transitions or
corridor-based dungeon structure. The player roams freely.
• Fixed forge located at a recognizable landmark; can be reached from different directions.
• Enemy packs, camps, trials, rifts and shrines use curated potential placement points, randomized or activated per run.
• Camera remains at current screenshot scale unless playtesting proves a moderate zoom change is necessary.
• Characters, demons and terrain use the three existing SmallScaleInt packs: HD 8-Directional Top-Down Character Pack
1; 2D Desert Tileset; Top-Down Demons.
• Environment should remain visually subordinate to combat telegraphs, loot and enemy silhouettes.
• Ground-level collision footprints and correct draw-depth order for props, rocks and tall structures.

03 Player controls and combat
CONTROL ABILITY EXPECTED BEHAVIOR
WASD Walk Eight-direction sprite movement independent of cursor.
Mouse Aim Precise world-space aim, nearest visual eight-direction facing.
LMB Sword combo Manual 3-hit combo; may continue by holding button.
RMB Heavy Cleave Slower, broad attack for stagger and large targets.
Q Parry Brief skill-timed counter window; some boss moves un-parryable.
E Ground Slam AoE damage/stagger, crowd management.
R Blade Storm Powerful player-triggered ultimate.
SPACE Dash Directional evade; pass through small enemies.
I / F Inventory / interact Pause solo game in menus; interact with forge/events.
Combat feel - nonnegotiable
• No automatic basic attacks or abilities: player decides when to strike.
• Input buffer allows smooth combos. Dash can cancel specific recovery windows without removing all attack
commitment.
• Attacks hit based on precise mouse direction, not merely snapped sprite facing.
• Attack hit windows must align with provided animation frames; small hitstop, distinct parry sound and stagger
reactions.
• Maintain clear melee openings on bosses; no required skill-node or weapon to beat them.
Prototype numbers - TEST FIRST
250 starting HP; combo hits 45/50/65; Heavy Cleave 110; movement 220 units/sec; dash approximately 3-second
cooldown and 0.18-second invulnerability; ultimate around 35-second cooldown. These are tuning seeds, not confirmed
balance.

04 Enemy roles, crowds and collision
ROLE START HP TACTICAL FUNCTION
Swarmer 75 Fast frequent close-range pressure; approach around player.
Bruiser 225 Slow wide telegraphed attacks; punish standing still.
Caster 100 Keeps distance; fires dodgeable patterns.
Charger 150 Visible windup then committed dash trajectory.
Guardian 450 Large sturdy blocker; protects other enemies.
Summoner 175 Stays back; creates capped reinforcements.
Locked collision model
• Regular enemies use soft separation, may overlap slightly and cannot hard-trap the player.
• Only large demons, elite leaders and bosses body-block the player. Terrain is solid.
• Dash ignores ordinary-enemy bodies but collides with terrain and major blockers.
• Collision uses footprint near feet, never the entire image bounding rectangle.
• Enemy steering combines pursuit, spacing and obstacle avoidance with capped separation impulses.
Attack-pressure system
Approx. 3-4 regular melee enemies may initiate their strike at once as an initial readability target; others circle, move
and reposition. Ranged enemies, elites and bosses use separate encounter coordination. Already-started animations
complete unless interrupted by valid game rules.
Performance target
Use spatial hashing, pooled enemy/projectile objects, lightweight group steering, AI update throttling and shared
animation textures. Prototype toward 150-180 active enemies at the late-run peak while preserving input response. FPS
benchmarks are goals, not verified performance.

05 Run pacing and encounter director
Confirmed: enemies spawn continuously across the desert. There are no mandatory downtime rounds. Difficulty
gradually escalates with timed peaks, shifting mixtures of regular monsters and increasingly threatening encounters.
RUN TIME POPULATION TARGET NEW PRESSURE
0-5 min 12-40 Swarmers, bruisers, early elites.
5-10 min 40-70 First major boss; caster threats.
10-15 min 70-100 Chargers and mixed attacks.
15-20 min 100-140 Guardians, summoners, Blood Moon proposed.
20-25 min 140-165 Elite-heavy packs and difficult specials.
25-30 min 165-180 Climax, major boss; potential endless continuation.
Spawn constraints
• Spawn ordinary demons outside the visible camera in valid navigable locations; avoid obvious pop-in.
• Use a regenerating spawn budget and enemy-cost weights; do not instantly fill all killed positions.
• Never spawn a boss directly on top of the player; limit simultaneous major bosses and queue unresolved encounters.
• Normal enemies can be recycled far from action; never despawn an actively engaged boss or elite.
• Consider an approx. 30-minute core run, then optional endless mode - the ending condition remains open.

06 Elite packs, mini-bosses and major bosses
Elite packs - gold hunting
One leader plus roughly 3-6 supporting demons; modifiers can include Explosive, Vampiric, Frostbound, Shielded,
Berserker and Warding. Pack leaders have recognizable tells and visual auras. Baseline approximately one pack every
60-90 seconds; 40-80 gold and ~15% weapon drop chance proposed.
Mini-bosses - strategic skill points
Powerful roaming or landmark encounters around every ~3 minutes as a starting target. They grant one skill-tree point
on eligible kills without automatically opening any menu. Cap skill-point-producing wins around 8-10 per run; future
mini-bosses award gold/loot, not endless skill points. Exact spawning and point cap are untested.
Major bosses - mechanics, not stat checks
BOSS ATTACK DESIGN REWARD
Ashen Butcher (~5 min) Sweeps, delayed charge, ground smash; phase shift at 50% HP. Guaranteed weapon + gold.
Hollow Prophet (~10 min) Projectile fans, marked eruptions, reposition, summoned guardians. Guaranteed weapon + gold.
Boss acceptance criteria: at least two genuinely learnable mechanics beyond damage output; distinguish parryable
moves; clear telegraphs, reasonable punish windows and a viable melee approach; no impossible overlapping hazard
sequences. Adapt inspiration from V Rising and RuneScape encounter design.

09 Evolving weapons: permanent, but balanced
NEW CONFIRMED RULE: after each ordinary death, start the next run with the selected permanent weapon at effective
level 1
. Its numerical power increases with character level until it reaches that weapon's saved maximum item level /
power ceiling. The weapon does not permanently lose its original ceiling.
WEAPON PROPERTY START OF NEXT RUN
Name, rarity, family, saved max level Kept.
Visual style, blade aura, signature effect identity Kept and visible from level 1.
Affix types / rolled quality / forged improvements Kept as persistent data.
Current effective weapon level Reset to 1.
Base damage / numeric affix magnitude Scaled to level 1, then progressively restored.
Full saved max-level power Reached when player level catches weapon ceiling.
Weapon XP / weapon mastery None - no usage-based level-ups.
Example: a saved level-25 sword
At player level 1, the sword behaves numerically like its level-1 counterpart. At player level 10, its effective level is 10.
At player level 25 and beyond, its effective level is capped at 25. The sword's red flame, name and special attack
behavior remain present throughout; damage and proc coefficients scale. This is character-level synchronization, not
weapon mastery.
Crucial balance test
Decide the exact level-1 normalization curve and whether some signature effects need minimum proc magnitude limits.
Preserve functional identity without granting late-game damage immediately. Forging upgrades should affect its
full-power ceiling and scale proportionally in early levels.

10 Loot, affixes and drops
Drop pipeline: choose loot eligibility > weapon family > rarity > maximum item level > base roll > compatible affixes
and signature > visual treatment > unique saved instance.
AFFIX POOL EXAMPLES
Physical offense Physical damage, attack speed, crit chance, crit damage, elite damage.
Defensive Max HP, damage reduction while attacking, recovery after kill.
Mobility Dash cooldown, dash range, movement speed.
Elemental Fire / ignite, frost / chill, lightning / shock.
Status Bleed, poison, debuff duration, bonuses to afflicted targets.
Ability-specific Cleave radius, Ground Slam damage, parry bonus, ultimate duration.
Utility Gold find, XP gain, pickup radius, limited cooldown recovery.
Drop philosophy and starting odds
Basic demons ~0.15% weapon drop chance, elites ~15%, major bosses always drop a weapon. For initial boss rarity
tests: Common 15%, Magic 40%, Rare 30%, Legendary 12%, Mythic 2.5%, Ascendant 0.5%. These probabilities are
proposals and should be checked against actual enemies killed per run.
Rules to prevent runaway builds
• No duplicate or mutually incompatible affixes; signature effects draw from curated pools.
• Affix tiers depend on the weapon's stored item level; better tier eligibility does not guarantee better rolls.
• Add ordinary bonuses within explicit groups; strictly control multiplicative procs, attack speed and cooldown reductions.
• Keep actual randomized rolls in permanent save data, so balance changes do not silently regenerate player items.

14 Wandering merchant - APPROVED FEATURE
A merchant intermittently appears in a sensible desert location, remains for a limited time, and sells weapons for
permanent gold. Merchant access provides an alternative to random drops without undermining boss hunting.
SLOT OFFER PLAYER TRADEOFF
1 Affordable randomized weapon Low-risk spend / basic upgrade.
2 Higher-rarity or higher-ceiling weapon Expensive but inspectable.
3 Wildcard or sealed weapon Unknown affix details; gamble on quality.
Merchant rules
• Initial appearance estimate: ~20-30% of runs. This is provisional.
• Stock changes on each appearance; no guaranteed Ascendant or exclusive Sovereign loot.
• Prices scale with rarity and maximum weapon level; purchases are permanent.
• Vendor and weapon purchase transaction must be saved reliably.
• Consider merchant voice lines and rare ominous hints about the Sovereign without revealing a guaranteed arrival.

15 Demonic rifts - APPROVED FEATURE
A demonic portal opens in the desert and starts producing monsters. The player can ignore it or enter the danger to
destroy its anchors before time runs out. This is a target-priority event, not simply another oversized health bar.
Proposed encounter sequence
STEP MECHANIC
1. Rift opens Rift appears at valid location with readable map marker.
2. Three anchors Player must attack independently placed anchors.
3. Escalation Rift spawns progressively more dangerous waves until sealed.
4. Success Destroy anchors before ~90 sec: gold + weapon chance.
5. Failure Bonus reward lost; normal run continues after rift collapses.
Blood Moon interaction
Rifts opened during a Blood Moon use faster, rarer demons and increase rewards, but remain avoidable. Spawn caps
and local pressure budgets prevent a rift from spawning unlimited entities alongside a scheduled major boss.

18 Secret realm abduction - APPROVED FEATURE
Signature feature: an extremely rare, mechanically demanding boss may abduct the player without the player opting in.
The encounter may occur at unpredictable times and does not happen every run.
RULE DESIGN
Arrival Rare random event; proposed ~15-25% of eligible runs; no guarantee.
Frequency Maximum one abduction per run as starting constraint.
Transition Freeze world, preserve player location and active main-realm state.
Victorious player Receives one exclusive Sovereign weapon and returns to original world.
Defeated player Returns to original world and resumes ordinary run - NOT game over.
Encounter duration Separate realm timer; original day/night, buff and boss clocks pause.
Return safety Brief protected entry window to avoid instant damage from existing enemies.
The Hollow Sovereign: mechanically challenging
PHASE ATTACK / PUZZLE
1. Duel, 100-75% Delayed sword sequence; recognize parryable and dodge-only blows.
2. Fractured Floor, 75-50% Sequential safe zones and delayed exploding floor runes.
3. Mirror Trial, 50-25% Discriminate true boss from copies using repeatable tells.
4. Reckoning, 25-0% Combines learned mechanics with tightly controlled overlaps.
The boss must be hard because of timing, positioning, pattern recognition and decision-making - not simply an absurd
HP pool or unfair one-shot damage. It is beatable with ordinary equipment and a well-played baseline skill kit.

20 Event director and safeguards

Fairness / performance
• At most two active optional timed world events as an initial cap.
 
• Pause the main realm in a Sovereign fight; return to preserved state without timing exploits.
 
• Do not generate event objectives inside inaccessible props or behind blocked geometry.
 
• A single event must never force impossible overlap between multiple mechanics.
 
• Keep optional world events discoverable through subtle landmarks/map hints, not constant full-screen prompts.

Engineering guardrails
• Do not rebuild the existing Codex project or map without first auditing current framework, sprites, animation sheets and
scene code.
• Use data-driven definitions for enemy behavior, events, weapons, affixes, effects and boss patterns.
• Version permanent weapon saves and make gold spending + item updates transactional.
• Separate UI from simulation; inventory, forge and skill tree pause solo gameplay.
• Use animation pooling, atlases and benchmark dense enemy scenes on real browsers.
• Implement automated tests for weapon rescaling, realm return after death, exclusive drops, forge atomicity and event
scheduling.
Open decisions
• 30-minute victory condition vs purely endless survival.
• Whether late-game buffs can be renewed or only refreshed, and whether camp buffs stack across categories.
• How much health the Sovereign returns the player with after defeat.
• Exact skill point cap and whether some trials may rarely grant bonus points.
• Whether forging costs depend on item ceiling, current quality and signature rarity.
• Whether merchant has a safe interaction bubble (currently not established).
