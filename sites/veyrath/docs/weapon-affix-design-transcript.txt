 VEYRATH | WEAPON AFFIX SYSTEM
V0.5 Quick Reference - Warrior + Mage | Codex companion specification
Core rule: Weapon family determines only the LMB basic-attack pattern. Ordinary affixes enhance its numbers or behavior. RMB, Q, E,
R, and SPACE are class skills, unchanged by ordinary weapon affixes. Red Ascendant weapons alone may carry a separate signature
effect that changes other skills.

6 | Compatibility and safeguard rules
No duplicate IDs, incompatible opposing behaviors, or more than two behavior affixes. No recursive extra attacks or unbounded
projectile spawning. Limit repeated target hits and on-hit procs. Examples: Focused Pages conflicts with Scattered Pages; Expanded
Pulse conflicts with Focused Pulse; Returning Axe conflicts with Ricochet initially; third-hit Longsword replacements are exclusive.
7 | Loot generation
1. Roll family, rarity and item-level ceiling. 2. Roll ordinary-affix count. 3. Roll 0-2 eligible behavior affixes within rarity cap. 4. Fill
remaining slots with compatible stat affixes. 5. Roll values and validate exclusions. 6. Ascendant only: roll separately curated exclusive
signature. 7. Save unique instance, rolls, quality, forge history, visual rarity and schema version.

9 | Scaling, combat balance and persistence

VEYRATH  /  Weapon Affix System V0.5
3
Effective level = min(character level, weapon item-level ceiling). Base damage and numerical affix values scale upward; behavioral
identities (e.g. +2 pierce, second pulse) remain active at level 1. A proposed starting model scales percentage bonuses from 40% of
their full rolled value at level 1 to 100% at ceiling. Weapons retain their appearances, affixes and signature identity after death; gold
persists; no weapon mastery XP.
Balance with single-target boss tests, dense-horde clear tests, and moving elite-pack tests. Tune extra projectile damage, echo
damage, hit records, proc caps, attack rates and VFX budgets so behavior-affix combinations do not multiply damage uncontrollably.
10 | Codex implementation priorities
Data-driven WeaponFamily, AffixDefinition, WeaponInstance and BasicAttack models; deterministic modifier ordering (base > shape >
projectile count > travel/pierce > hit > secondary effects); weighted eligible rolls; validation; atomic forge/save transactions; automated
tests for affix caps, incompatibility, level-reset scaling, red-only signatures, and ability-slot separation.
Supersedes: Earlier proposed fixed rarity-breakpoint modifiers. Preserves: Veyrath world design, per-five-level specialization points,
fixed forge, weapon/gold persistence, and Mage Arcane Energy design. Note: All numbers and affix pools here are provisional until
playtested.
