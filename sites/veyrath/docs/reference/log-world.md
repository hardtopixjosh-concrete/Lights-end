### October 15 — no hard stops on obstacles: player slide + smoother enemy steering (Claude session, not published)
User: collision should not hard-stop the character; both the player and enemies should move around obstacles. New `slideMove` (simulation.js) drives player walking: it takes the input step and, if mostly blocked, glides along the obstacle with the smallest deflection (0.35-1.65 rad, preferring the last successful side) that moves at least 45% of the step without going backward; it never auto-paths, so changing direction always tries the input first. `steerMove` (enemies/bosses) now also tries a small 0.35 rad detour first, and ranged casters' repositioning uses it too (pull effects such as Vortex/Sandstorm still push into walls by design). Dash is unchanged. Scratch test on the new map pushing straight into 438 real obstacles (boulders, trees, cacti, walls, stalls, bones, statues) from four directions for 2s: old movement got past 0, player slide 306 (70%; most remaining are long walls the player must steer along), enemy steering 391 (89%). Full suite: same 16 pre-existing failing files. Tags `fx-27`.
