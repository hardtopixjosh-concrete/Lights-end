## Current rules — V0.9.2 implementation, October 15

The user adopted `VEYRATH_V0_9_2_COMPLETE_UPDATE_WITH_WARRIOR.pdf` (14 pages) and explicitly confirmed retaining the recent 100-damage Longsword baseline, superseding the PDF's older 250 example. Read [V0.9.2 implementation report](docs/V0_9_2_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md) and relevant original PDF pages before further changes. All C01–C07 change-order features and the Warrior appendix are implemented. Older five-level/instant-XP/escalating-forge/small-tree notes below are historical wherever they conflict with this checkpoint.

Weapon family controls LMB only. Six Warrior families retain their recent damage/timings and gain 10% reach/travel. Mage basics remain free and arcane-colored. Choose one of four paths per class at level 1: Warrior Berserker/Vanguard/Spellblade/Warlord; Mage Pyromancer/Cryomancer/Stormcaller/Blood Mage. RMB/Q/E/R use the selected kit; SPACE Dash/Blink remains class movement. Walking faces WASD; attacks aim with mouse. Ordinary affixes remain LMB-scoped, with permitted passive stats; red-only signatures remain restricted and no curated signatures are introduced.

**Leveling (October 15, replaces the 36-node trees/talent points):** runs start at level 1 with a spell choice right away (only LMB and Dash/Blink before it); levels 5 and 10 each grant another spell (choice among the remaining RMB/Q/E). Level 15 grants the R spell. Then every 5th level (20/25/30) offers a spell behavior (4 standalone behaviors per spell, no tree). Every other level offers 3 stat/utility cards. Mini-bosses grant up to 5 extra behavior picks ("Spell insight"). Healing: 0.8 HP/s regen, random heart HP packs, one rechargeable healing pool. Mage spells use short cooldowns with higher energy costs (mana-limited); Warrior abilities spend Rage (built by hitting and being hit) with short cooldowns. The K panel is a read-only Spellbook; respec is removed. See decisions.md and log-systems.md. Mini-boss rewards are deferred, unique choices of spell insight/gold/temporary blessing, with a survival alternative after the bonus cap. Ground XP crystals replace automatic kill XP. Upper-right minimap shows nearby terrain and discovered encounters/forge. Reforge prices are flat per rarity and operation; paid old/new choices persist until resolved atomically. Gold persists; weapons found in a run are lost when it ends except the equipped one and the one-slot safe pocket (Oct 17, see decisions.md); spells, cards, pending picks, buffs and uncollected XP reset with the run.

Current scope and limitations are recorded in the report. October 15 world-events batch adds cursed shrines, guarded buff camps, wandering merchant and optional rifts with discovery markers. See docs/WORLD_EVENTS_IMPLEMENTATION.md for actual rules and prototype values. Trials, major/realm bosses and day/night/Blood Moon remain future milestones. The single desert battlefield, forge, waves, elites and Dune Ravager continue. Actual tuning remains provisional where the PDF says PROPOSED.

**Run structure (October 16 overnight batch, fx-60; see docs/ROADMAP.md):** a 30-minute run. Day 0–12, night 12–20, Blood Moon 20–24, dusk 24–30. Main bosses: Ashen Butcher 7:30, Hollow Prophet 15:00, Obsidian Colossus 22:30. At 30:00 the arena clears and the final boss, the Crimson Tyrant, arrives (6 mechanics, victory screen and Legendary weapon). The Dune Ravager and Dune Wyrm are optional pit bosses on the outskirts that grant buffs. New enemies: Charger, Guardian, Summoner. Ancient Trials colosseum. Within-kit elemental reactions. Optional events scale with the run clock. 36 stat cards were replaced by real spell behaviors. Playtest with `?debug`.

## How we work

Latest publish-only preference (October 19, user): when asked to update/publish completed files, upload changed files and activate the release without additional sound, gameplay, smoke, syntax or visual checks. Minimize tool calls, reading and usage. Retain required content hashing, upload-receipt matching and native deployment confirmation. Investigate only an actual upload/deployment failure. This supersedes earlier optional pre-publication checks; implementation/testing requests are separate.

October 19 delivery rule: original media quality is mandatory. Updates use the exact-byte incremental workflow in [ASSET_DELIVERY.md](docs/ASSET_DELIVERY.md), from the isolated publisher. An unchanged release skips deployment. This supersedes the full-media archive workaround.

Read this index before game changes, then only the segment(s) below that cover the area you are changing, then the relevant PDF pages. Read every segment only when the user asks for a full-game review. New decisions made with the user update this file and its decision history. Latest explicit user decisions take precedence over earlier material. PDF annotations take precedence over printed rules they amend. Proposed numbers remain tuning seeds until tested and approved.

Latest workflow (October 14): build larger, coherent feature batches for faster playable progress, with polish later. This supersedes the previous one-small-ability-per-review workflow. Bundle related systems and publish once per batch; do not wait for separate reviews after every tiny feature. Maintain this reference and follow the PDF/chat decisions. The PDF remains the destination; prioritize the functional game loop before fine polish.

Testing preference, latest October 14: essentials only per batch—one focused integration smoke check, necessary syntax/asset checks and a brief visual check if useful. Broader checks only for a concrete failure or major integration risk. Do not repeat successful checks in packaging or duplicate local/production passes. Keep source/archive and deployment confirmation.

Use the actual character and environment assets immediately. Validate movement, facing, depth and collision before combat. Blocks/circles may be used as visible debug overlays; replacing the established art style is unnecessary.

Latest publishing preference (October 17): finished Claude updates use the fast publish-only workflow—this index plus newest relevant notes, reference sync and packaging/deployment confirmation; reuse recorded checks and defer deeper catch-up until implementation/review.
